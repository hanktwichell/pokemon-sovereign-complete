# What the game takes from Drayano's hacks

Two of Drayano's ROM hacks, used with his okay: **Sacred Gold** (Johto and Kanto, read from its ROM: `docs/SacredGold.md`)
and **Renegade Platinum** (Sinnoh, through Sovereign Platinum's engine, whose changes say "as in Renegade" in the code).
For the credits; kept up to date as the owner's calls change it.

## Sacred Gold (Johto and Kanto)

- **Trainers:** all 737 of HeartGold's trainers with Sacred Gold's teams (species, levels, held items, moves,
  abilities, difficulty, AI): every Gym Leader, the Elite Four, Lance, Red, the rival and the rematches. 68 trainer
  classes Platinum doesn't have, with its pictures.
- **Scripts and story:** its 203 changed script files (gifts, trades, rematches, item givers), 201 more people on the
  maps, its text (533 banks; Pokémon names in plain case), the S.S. Aqua's first voyage and cabin rest.
- **Gift Pokémon:** Cynthia's Eevee by Mr. Pokémon's house, Bill's Castform (with a Mystic Water) for HeartGold's
  Eevee, and its other changed gifts and starters.
- **Trades:** its versions of HeartGold's 13 (a Gligar for a Rhyhorn, a Torkoal for a Noctowl, a Rhyperior for a Bonsly,
  a Beldum for a Gible, a Skarmory for an Electabuzz, a Porygon2 for a Quagsire, a Chansey for a Munchlax, an Espeon for
  a Clefable, a Smoochum for a Mime Jr....), each giving a Covenant Orb.
- **Items:** six new evolution items (Covenant Orb for trade evolutions, Voltaic Ore, Woodland Ore, Chilled Ore, Aubade
  Orb, Serenade Orb); its item balls (19 changed, 15 placed or moved) and 2 more hidden items; 14 special Poké Marts'
  stock (Goldenrod's and Celadon's TMs, Mahogany's weather TMs, stones in Goldenrod and Blackthorn...); Odd Keystones
  from the S.S. Aqua's captain.
- **Special encounters** (its Special Event Guide): Rotom (New Bark's TV), Spiritomb (Slowpoke Well), Friday Lapras,
  Sudowoodo, the red Gyarados and Snorlax at its levels and spots; the shiny pseudo-legend radio in Tohjo Falls;
  Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres (Cinnabar Gym), Mewtwo and the "???" trainer; Mew (Viridian Forest), Celebi (Azure Flute),
  the Regis (Lock Capsule), the Soul Dew's Lati, Groudon, Kyogre and Rayquaza at its levels, Deoxys (Route 3), Phione,
  and Arceus with its 16-plate treasure hunt at the Sinjoh Ruins (`docs/Legendaries.md`).
- **Numbers:** Johto's and Kanto's level caps (its aces' levels) and the Totem trials' levels there.
- **Not taken:** its wild Pokémon (HeartGold's and SoulSilver's own since Nov 1), its Pokémon data and moves
  (Sovereign's 1025), the trade items made usable from the bag (open). **Taken out:** its Cresselia and Darkrai, Jirachi,
  Shaymin, Manaphy Egg, Heatran, Regigigas and the Sinjoh Ruins' trios (Nov 6: met in Sinnoh instead).

## Renegade Platinum (Sinnoh, through Sovereign)

- **Trainers:** every Sinnoh trainer's team from its trainer list, at levels moved up as Sinnoh comes third; one fixed
  team each for the Elite Four and Cynthia (from its four random ones); its added battles: the rival on Routes 202, 207,
  210 and 224, Cheryl, Marley, Riley, Buck, Dahlia, Cyrus's Dialga-and-Palkia double, Darach, the six Battle Zone
  leaders and their keepsake badges, the 17 Type Masters and their Plates.
- **Story and quality of life:** Mom gives the Bicycle; the Pokétch at once with every app; nurses in Victory Road, the
  fog and Route 221, and between Mars's and Jupiter's battles; Victory Road's back and Route 224 before the League; the
  Canalave road block; the Pokémon Mansion siege; the Snowpoint Temple before the Gym and Candice's HM08; Riley's HM04;
  free move tutors; the Move Deleter in Oreburgh; the training NPCs; traded Pokémon always obey.
- **Legendary events kept** (`docs/Legendaries.md`): Jirachi's three wishes, the Pokémon Mansion's Manaphy, Cresselia
  and Darkrai's islands (the Member Card from Route 217), Shaymin with Oak's Letter, Regigigas's temple story, the lake
  trio and Heatran at its levels. **Switched off** (Nov 6, HeartGold's or Sacred Gold's kept instead): its portals and
  quests for the beasts, the birds, Mewtwo, Mew, Celebi, Ho-Oh and Lugia, Kyogre, Groudon and Rayquaza, Deoxys, the
  Latis, the Regis and Arceus.
- **Items and TMs:** its TM and item givers (Return, Protect, Hidden Power, Focus Blast, Overheat, Scald, Shadow Ball,
  Earthquake, Ice Beam, Facade, Rest, Aerial Ace, False Swipe...); TMs as 99 that can't be sold; the evolution-item and
  Rare Berry sellers (₽10,000 each); its Game Corner prizes; the Veilstone store's stones; the Ice Rock's and Moss
  Rock's stones; its evolution items and Lv. 36 in place of trade evolutions.
- **Wild Pokémon:** its Honey Trees (their tables, its levels, Honey that works at once) and the Great Marsh's lookout.
  Sinnoh's other tables are Platinum's own with the new species since Nov 1.
